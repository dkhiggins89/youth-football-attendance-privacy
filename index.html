Privacy Policy — Youth Football Attendance

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Youth Football Attendance ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. The App is a mobile utility designed to assist adult youth-football coaches, club administrators, and managers in tracking training and match attendance.

By installing and using the App, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Summary of Core Principles

  • Local-First Architecture: Your squad data (player names, positions, attendance records, session summaries, and notes) is stored locally on your Android device using a private database. We do not run background servers, cloud databases, or custom synchronization APIs.
  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register, log in, or create an account to use the App.
  • Respect for Privacy: Since we do not transmit squad or attendance logs to our own servers, we cannot see, access, share, or sell your players' names or attendance records.

2. Information the App Collects & Processes

A. Local Data (Entered by You)

The App permits you to enter and store:

  • Team/Club names
  • Player names, jersey numbers, and field positions
  • Attendance registers (e.g., Present, Absent, Injured, Late)
  • Session details (e.g., date, type of session, custom notes)

How it is processed: This data is stored strictly in the App's private Android sandbox directory using an offline SQLite database. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the "CSV Export" feature to share files via other applications on your device (e.g., Email, WhatsApp, Google Drive).

The App's data is excluded from Google cloud backups. On Android 12 and later, if you move to a new phone using Android's device-to-device transfer (by cable or direct wireless connection), your squad data is copied directly from the old phone to the new one.

B. Third-Party Services (Google Services)

The App integrates industry-standard SDKs from Google to display advertisements, handle subscriptions, and manage user privacy choices. These services may collect and process telemetry data.

1. Google AdMob (Advertising)

To support development, the App displays anchored banner ads on selected overview screens (never during active attendance taking). If you have an active ad-free subscription, the App does not start AdMob or request ads at all. Otherwise, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID).
  • Usage metrics (ad impressions, clicks, interactions).
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address for regional targeting).
  • Device and Diagnostics data (manufacturer, OS version, performance logs).

2. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP)

To comply with global privacy regulations (such as GDPR, CCPA, and COPPA), we use Google’s UMP SDK to present a consent dialog. This service processes:

  • Your consent preferences (e.g., whether you accept personalized ads, non-personalized ads, or decline).
  • Device information to determine your geographic region (e.g., EEA, UK, California) to display the correct regulatory dialog.

3. Google Play Billing

To allow you to remove advertisements via a 1-year ad-free subscription, the App uses the official Google Play Billing Library. This library is securely managed by Google. Google processes your payment details, subscription status, and purchase history. The App only queries your device to check whether an active subscription is tied to your Google Play Account, storing a simple local preference (premium_active = true) to hide ads.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (EEA, UK, and Swiss Users)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, our processing of your information relies on the following legal bases:

  • Contractual Necessity: To provide the App's local tools and process active subscriptions via Google Play Billing.
  • Consent: For the delivery of personalized or non-personalized advertisements via Google AdMob and the collection of diagnostic identifiers, obtained via the Google UMP consent banner.
  • Legitimate Interests: To maintain App security, prevent fraud, and run performance diagnostics.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App is strictly intended for use by adults (coaches, managers, and club administrators over the age of 18). It is not designed or marketed for direct use by children.

Coaches and Club Responsibilities:

  • You are solely responsible for obtaining all necessary parental or guardian consents before entering any minor’s name or details into this App, in compliance with your local club rules, national associations, and local data protection regulations (e.g., GDPR, COPPA).
  • We do not collect or request any personal data from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). If we discover that child data has been transmitted to a third-party partner without proper legal authorization, we will coordinate to delete it immediately.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete individual player profiles, squad rosters, and session records directly within the App at any time.
  • Uninstalling the App: Because your registers are stored entirely on your device, uninstalling the App permanently deletes all local attendance databases.
  • Google Telemetry: AdMob and Billing logs are retained by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA, and Regional Laws)

Depending on your residency, you may have rights under the GDPR, CCPA, or similar state privacy frameworks, including the right to:

  • Access, Update, or Delete your personal choices.
  • Withdraw Consent: You can change or withdraw your consent choice at any time. To do this, tap the Settings (gear) icon at the top of any main screen, then tap Privacy & ad choices and modify your preferences. This option appears when a consent choice applies in your region.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can also manage personalized ads globally through your Android system settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads.

7. Security

The App relies on Android’s built-in application security sandbox to isolate local databases from other applications installed on your device. We encourage you to secure your physical device with biometric locks or PINs to protect local attendance details from unauthorized physical access.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.
